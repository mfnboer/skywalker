Child Safety Standards – Skywalker

Developer: Michel de Boer
Last updated: nov 16, 2026

Our position

Skywalker is a client app for the Bluesky social network (AT Protocol). We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any content that sexualises minors. These are prohibited when using Skywalker.

Moderation

Skywalker does not host user content. Posts and accounts are stored and moderated by Bluesky and other AT Protocol services. Bluesky’s rules are published in its community guidelines.

Reporting

Users can report posts and accounts from within Skywalker via ”post menu → Report post” or ”profile menu → Report account”. Reports are sent to the selected moderation service.

You can also report child safety concerns about the app itself by email to mfnboer+childsafety@gmail.com.

Our actions

If we become aware of CSAM or CSAE in connection with Skywalker, we will:

  • block or remove it in the app where possible;
  • report it to Bluesky and to the relevant authorities, such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the local reporting hotline or police;
  • cooperate with law enforcement as required by law.

We do not copy, store or forward such material ourselves.

Legal compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations, including those on reporting CSAM.
